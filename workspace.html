    Sunshine's Creative QA Rules 2026.10

    Help and known limits

    • This is a local preflight and review aid. It is not platform approval.
    • Empty limit fields use a general baseline. Only the CM360 preset comes from Google's documentation; DV360, Rich Media and Custom use the general baseline, so enter the real limits when you have them.
    • Click and exit detection combines static analysis with a sandboxed preview. It can miss unusual implementations, so always confirm in the destination platform's own preview.
    • Previews cannot reach external sites. Clicks, Enabler exits and page redirects are captured and shown instead.
    • Video and audio checks need the bundled FFmpeg tools in the desktop app.
